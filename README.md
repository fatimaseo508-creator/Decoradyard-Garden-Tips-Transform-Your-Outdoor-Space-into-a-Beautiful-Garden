<h2><strong><span style="font-size:17pt;font-family:Arial,sans-serif;">1. Plan Your Garden Layout</span></strong></h2>
<p><span style="font-size:11pt;font-family:Arial,sans-serif;">Before planting flowers or adding decorations, take some time to plan your garden layout. Consider the available space, sunlight, and the type of plants you want to grow. Divide your garden into different areas for flowers, vegetables, seating, or decorative features.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-size:11pt;font-family:Arial,sans-serif;">A well-organized layout makes your garden look neat and helps you use every corner effectively. You can also add pathways using stones, bricks, or gravel to create an attractive appearance.</span></p>
<h2><strong><span style="font-size:17pt;font-family:Arial,sans-serif;">2. Choose the Right Plants</span></strong></h2>
<p><span style="font-size:11pt;font-family:Arial,sans-serif;">Selecting suitable plants is one of the most important steps in gardening. Choose plants according to your local climate, soil conditions, and sunlight availability. Native plants are often easier to maintain because they naturally adapt to the environment.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-size:11pt;font-family:Arial,sans-serif;">Mix colorful flowers with green shrubs and small trees to create visual interest. Seasonal flowers can add freshness and variety throughout the year. For smaller gardens, consider growing plants in containers to save space.</span></p>
<h2><strong><span style="font-size:17pt;font-family:Arial,sans-serif;">3. Improve Your Garden Soil</span></strong></h2>
<p><span style="font-size:11pt;font-family:Arial,sans-serif;">Healthy soil is essential for strong plant growth. Add compost, organic fertilizer, or well-rotted manure to improve soil quality. These natural materials provide nutrients and help the soil retain moisture.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-size:11pt;font-family:Arial,sans-serif;">Before planting, remove weeds, stones, and unwanted debris. Loosen the soil so that plant roots can grow easily. Regular soil improvement will help your garden remain healthy and productive.</span></p>
<h2><strong><span style="font-size:17pt;font-family:Arial,sans-serif;">4. Add Attractive Garden Decorations</span></strong></h2>
<p><span style="font-size:11pt;font-family:Arial,sans-serif;">Garden decorations can make your outdoor space more enjoyable. Consider adding decorative pots, wooden benches, solar lights, or a small water fountain. These features create a relaxing atmosphere without requiring expensive renovations.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-size:11pt;font-family:Arial,sans-serif;">Choose decorations that complement your garden style. Simple and natural materials often create a timeless appearance.</span></p>
<h2><strong><span style="font-size:17pt;font-family:Arial,sans-serif;">5. Water Your Plants Properly</span></strong></h2>
<p><span style="font-size:11pt;font-family:Arial,sans-serif;">Watering is essential, but too much water can damage plants. Water your garden early in the morning to reduce evaporation. Check the soil before watering and adjust the amount according to the weather.</span></p>
<p><span style="font-size:11pt;font-family:Arial,sans-serif;">Using mulch around plants can help retain moisture, control weeds, and improve soil health.</span></p>
<h2><strong><span style="font-size:17pt;font-family:Arial,sans-serif;">6. Maintain Your Garden Regularly</span></strong></h2>
<p><span style="font-size:11pt;font-family:Arial,sans-serif;">Regular maintenance keeps your garden looking beautiful. Remove dead leaves, trim overgrown branches, and clean garden pathways. Check plants for pests and diseases so you can address problems early.</span></p>
